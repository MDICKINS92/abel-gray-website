Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through this website

Information We Collect

When you contact Abel Gray through this website, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, property or land location and the details of your enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, discuss the property or opportunity you have raised, and keep a record of our communication where necessary.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Enquiries submitted through this website are processed by Web3Forms, which passes the message to us. The contact form also uses hCaptcha to help prevent automated or abusive submissions. We may share relevant details with professional advisers or service providers where this is necessary to respond to your enquiry or progress a transaction.

Cookies and Security Services

This website uses essential cookies or similar technologies where needed to provide the contact form and protect it against spam and abuse. hCaptcha may process technical information, including your device and browser information, to provide that security service. We do not use advertising cookies or website analytics cookies.

Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as necessary to respond to the enquiry, meet legal or record-keeping obligations, or progress a transaction.

Contact

Abel Gray Homes Limited is the data controller for information submitted through this website. To ask about your information, contact info@abelgray.co.uk.
