What We Do

Based in Milton Keynes, Abel Gray acquires land and delivers individual homes and bespoke residential schemes across Buckinghamshire, Bedfordshire and Northamptonshire. We also invest in commercial property across London, the Midlands and South East. We are directly involved throughout each project, from the initial acquisition through to completion.

New Build Homes

We develop individual homes and small residential schemes with a strong focus on location, architecture, layout and finish. Each scheme is managed directly by us from acquisition and planning through to construction and handover.

Land & Planning

We acquire land with or without planning permission and work directly with landowners. This includes agricultural land, brownfield sites, redundant buildings, barns and smaller development opportunities. We can purchase outright or agree terms subject to securing planning.

Our Approach

01

Direct Involvement

We remain directly involved throughout our projects, from acquisition and planning through to funding, construction and completion.

02

Transparency & Honesty

We deal directly with landowners, purchasers, agents and professional teams, communicate clearly and do what we say we are going to do.

03

Planning Risk

Where land is acquired subject to planning, we fund and manage the planning process at our own cost and risk. The landowner is not expected to fund the application or professional team.

04

Discretion & Trust

Property transactions often require discretion. All opportunities and discussions are handled confidentially and professionally from the outset.
