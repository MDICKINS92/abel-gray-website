About This Development

A proposed collection of six detached homes in Great Addington, Northamptonshire.

The scheme will comprise two three bedroom homes, one four bedroom home and three five bedroom homes, each individually designed with generous gardens, private parking and landscaping forming an important part of the overall setting.

The layout has been designed to provide space between the properties and make the most of the site's rural position, with architecture and materials selected to sit naturally within the surrounding area.

Further information on individual homes, specifications, CGI images and release dates will be provided as the development progresses.
