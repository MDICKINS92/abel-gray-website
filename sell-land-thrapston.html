Land Opportunities Around Thrapston

Abel Gray acquires land for residential development across the Thrapston area. We consider land both with and without planning permission, including agricultural land, brownfield property, gardens, paddocks, development plots and redundant buildings.

We work directly with landowners and can agree an outright acquisition, conditional contract or Option Agreement depending on the site and circumstances. Where a transaction is subject to planning, we can fund and manage the planning process ourselves.

North Northamptonshire Council is the local planning authority. Its local plan policies and published housing land supply evidence are reviewed for each opportunity, together with the Nene Valley landscape, village setting, access and heritage considerations.

Local Context

The villages around Thrapston commonly bring forward stone barns, redundant rural buildings, paddocks and smaller sites close to established settlements. Pepys Lane in nearby Slipton is a completed Abel Gray residential scheme.
