Land Opportunities Around Thrapston

Abel Gray considers land and property with residential development potential around Thrapston and the Nene Valley. We welcome confidential discussions with owners of sites that may suit a small residential scheme, whether or not planning permission is already in place.

The area includes established villages, river-valley settlements and edge-of-village opportunities. We assess planning context, access, landscape setting and the ability to deliver a proportionate scheme before proposing a route forward.

Areas We Cover

Our Thrapston area coverage includes Thrapston, Slipton, Oundle, Brigstock, Denford, Warmington, Cotterstock, Achurch, Woodford, Twywell, Fotheringhay, Aldwincle, Glapthorn, Deenethorpe, Titchmarsh and Great Addington.

Land with Potential

Agricultural land, paddocks, gardens and infill opportunities that may be suitable for residential development.

Previously Used Sites

Brownfield land, redundant buildings and under-used property that may benefit from a new residential purpose.

Local Planning Context

We consider sites with consent as well as opportunities requiring a planning strategy, with particular care for settlement character and the rural setting.
