Land Opportunities Around Thrapston

The Nene Valley villages around Thrapston are where Abel Gray has done most of its building, so this is familiar ground. We buy land here with or without planning: paddocks, gardens, farmland, stone barns, redundant farm buildings and plots within the villages themselves.

We can buy outright, buy subject to planning, or agree an Option. If a site needs consent, we appoint the planning consultant and architect and pay for the work, so there is no cost to you in testing whether a site has a future.

North Northamptonshire Council is the local planning authority. Its local plan policies and published housing land supply evidence are reviewed for each opportunity, together with the Nene Valley landscape, village setting, access and heritage considerations.

Thrapston Area Villages

Around Thrapston: Thrapston, Islip, Denford, Titchmarsh, Twywell, Woodford, Ringstead, Great Addington, Little Addington and Slipton.

Towards Oundle: Oundle, Barnwell, Pilton, Wadenhoe, Aldwincle, Achurch, Lilford, Clopton, Stoke Doyle, Glapthorn, Cotterstock, Fotheringhay and Warmington.

The Rockingham Forest edge: Brigstock, Sudborough, Lowick, Thorpe Waterville, Deenethorpe, Grafton Underwood and Cranford.

Towards Raunds: Raunds, Chelveston and Hargrave.

Local Context
