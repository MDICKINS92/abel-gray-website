Residential Homes

Abel Gray develops a small number of carefully considered homes, from individual country residences to modest collections of houses. Each scheme begins with the character of its setting and is managed closely from acquisition through to completion.

Our focus is practical planning, coherent architecture and durable materials. We work with experienced consultants and trades to deliver homes that are comfortable to live in and appropriate to their surroundings.
