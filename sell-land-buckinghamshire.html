Land Opportunities in Buckinghamshire

Residential development land and property considered by Abel Gray

Our Buckinghamshire Focus

We consider land, redundant buildings, gardens and small development sites in Buckinghamshire where a residential scheme can respond appropriately to its setting.

Buckinghamshire Council is the local planning authority. Local policy, settlement context and published housing land supply evidence are reviewed for each opportunity; a discussion with Abel Gray is not a planning opinion or a commitment to purchase.

What We Consider

Land and Gardens

Land adjoining settlements, gardens and infill opportunities with a potential residential use.

Redundant Buildings

Farm buildings and previously used sites that may have a credible future residential role.

Project Context

We develop a small number of projects and assess every opportunity directly from our Northamptonshire base.
