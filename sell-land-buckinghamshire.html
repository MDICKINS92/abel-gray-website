Land Opportunities in Buckinghamshire

Residential development land and property considered by Abel Gray

Our Buckinghamshire Focus

Abel Gray acquires land for residential development across Buckinghamshire. We consider land both with and without planning permission, including agricultural land, brownfield property, gardens, paddocks, development plots and redundant buildings.

We work directly with landowners and can agree an outright acquisition, conditional contract or Option Agreement depending on the site and circumstances. Where a transaction is subject to planning, we can fund and manage the planning process ourselves.

Buckinghamshire Council is the principal local planning authority. Its local plan policy and published housing land supply evidence are considered alongside conservation, transport, landscape and settlement matters for the particular site.

Local Context

Around Buckingham, Milton Keynes and the Vale, opportunities include village infill, garden land, rural buildings and smaller sites close to established services. We look at the individual location rather than applying a fixed site model.
