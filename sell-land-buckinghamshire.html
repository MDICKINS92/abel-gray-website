Our Buckinghamshire Focus

Abel Gray is based in Milton Keynes and buys land for new homes across Buckinghamshire. A site does not need planning permission before we get involved. We look at farmland, paddocks, gardens, brownfield property, redundant buildings and plots that already have consent.

Being local means we can usually visit a site quickly and give you a straight answer on whether it interests us. Depending on what suits you, we can buy outright, buy subject to planning, or agree an Option Agreement. Where planning is needed, we appoint and pay for the professional team ourselves.

Two authorities cover this area. Buckinghamshire Council is the planning authority for Buckingham, Winslow, the Vale and the south of the county. Milton Keynes City Council covers Milton Keynes and its surrounding villages, including Olney, Newport Pagnell, Stony Stratford and Woburn Sands. We review the relevant local plan and housing land supply position for whichever authority applies to your site.
