Commercial Property Investment

Direct commercial property investment across London, the Midlands and South East.

What We Invest In

We acquire commercial property directly for our own account, with a focus on assets where we understand the location, income and underlying property value.

Our interests range from established income producing investments to vacant or underperforming buildings where refurbishment, reletting, change of use or redevelopment may provide a stronger future use.

We are able to assess opportunities quickly and deal directly with owners, agents and their professional advisers.

Frequently Asked Questions About Selling Commercial Property

What types of commercial property do you buy?

We consider a wide range of commercial property including retail, mixed use, leisure, healthcare and other income producing or redevelopment assets.

Do you buy commercial property with tenants in place?

Yes. We acquire both tenanted investments and vacant or owner occupied properties.

How quickly can you complete a commercial property purchase?

This depends on the property and legal work required. As we are buying directly, decisions can be made quickly once we have assessed the opportunity.

Are there any fees to sell my commercial property to you?

There are no agents' fees payable to Abel Gray where we are buying directly from the owner. Each party remains responsible for its own professional and legal costs unless agreed otherwise.

What areas do you invest in?

Our principal area covers London, the Midlands and South East.

Do you buy commercial property that needs refurbishment?

Yes. Refurbishment, reletting and repositioning opportunities are an important part of what we consider.
