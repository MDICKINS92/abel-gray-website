Asset Management

Managing and improving property we own.

Our Approach

Not every Abel Gray acquisition is intended for immediate development or resale. We retain commercial and residential property where there is a strong reason to continue owning it.

That may involve improving the existing income, agreeing a new lease, refurbishing the property, securing a different planning use or simply holding an asset where we believe the underlying property has further value.

These decisions are made directly within the business, with Matthew remaining involved in the strategy for each property.

How We Manage Property

Planning & Consent

Where planning can materially improve an asset, we appoint and manage the professional team and fund the process directly.

Development & Delivery

Where physical works are required, we manage the design, specification, construction and completion of those works.

Leasing & Repositioning

We work with existing and prospective occupiers to improve income and ensure the property remains commercially relevant.

Retention

Some properties are retained for income rather than sold following improvement, particularly where we see value in continued ownership.

Property We Own and Manage

Our property includes:

  • Commercial investments
  • Mixed use property
  • Residential development sites
  • Properties requiring refurbishment or reletting
  • Buildings with planning or redevelopment prospects
  • Completed property retained for income
