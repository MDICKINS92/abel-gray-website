Asset Management

How we manage, improve and grow our own property portfolio

Our Approach

Abel Gray maintains an active portfolio of residential and commercial assets. Rather than acquiring and moving on, we take a considered, long-term view of each asset — managing, improving and repositioning properties within our portfolio to protect and grow their value over time.

Each acquisition is assessed not just on its immediate potential, but on the longer-term opportunity it presents. For some assets, that means straightforward residential development. For others, it involves planning strategy, change of use, refurbishment, or repositioning before sale or retention as a longer-term income-producing investment.

Matthew remains directly involved in asset management decisions across the portfolio — from reviewing individual assets to setting the direction for each project within the context of the wider business.

How We Manage Our Portfolio

Planning & Consent

We fund and manage the planning process on our own sites, taking the risk on consent where we see genuine long-term value in the opportunity.

Development & Delivery

Where we develop, we manage the process directly — from design and specification through to construction, completion and handover.

Repositioning

Some assets within the portfolio are acquired with a change of use or repositioning strategy in mind — improving their income potential or preparing them for future development.

Income & Retention

Where appropriate, we retain assets as longer-term income-producing investments, managing them actively to maintain performance and protect long-term value.

Asset Types Within Our Portfolio

Our portfolio includes a range of residential and commercial assets:

  • Residential development sites and completed homes
  • Former commercial buildings with residential or mixed-use potential
  • Income-producing commercial investments
  • Sites with planning consent or longer-term planning potential
  • Assets acquired for repositioning or change of use
