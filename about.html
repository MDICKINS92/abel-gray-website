About Matthew Dickins

Matthew Dickins is the Director of Abel Gray, a privately owned property development and investment company named after his two sons, Abel and Grayson. The business was established to focus on Matthew's passion for property investment and bespoke residential development.

Matthew remains directly involved in each acquisition and development, from initial appraisal and planning strategy through to construction, letting or sale. His focus is residential development and commercial investment — identifying the right opportunities, managing the planning and delivery process, and ensuring each project reflects the values the business was built on.
