Our Company

Abel Gray Homes Limited is a privately owned property company named after the founder’s two sons, Abel and Grayson. We focus on residential development, land acquisition and commercial property investment.

Each opportunity is considered directly, from initial appraisal and planning strategy through to construction, retention or sale. Our work is centred on sound locations, a practical understanding of the planning process and careful delivery.
