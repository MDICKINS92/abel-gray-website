Residential development land and property considered by Abel Gray

Our Northamptonshire Focus

Abel Gray operates from Northamptonshire and considers a limited number of land and property opportunities across the county. We assess agricultural land, gardens, redundant buildings, brownfield sites and infill plots where there is a credible route to a proportionate residential scheme.

Northamptonshire is administered by West Northamptonshire Council and North Northamptonshire Council. Relevant local plan policies and housing land supply evidence are considered at the time of each appraisal; no planning outcome is assumed.
