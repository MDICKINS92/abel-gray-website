Our Northamptonshire Focus

Northamptonshire is where Abel Gray has built and is building, so we know the county well. We buy land for new homes across it, with or without planning permission, including agricultural land, paddocks, gardens, brownfield sites, redundant buildings and consented plots.

We deal with landowners directly rather than through a chain of agents. Terms can be an outright purchase, a contract conditional on planning, or an Option Agreement. Where planning is required, we run and fund the process ourselves.

Northamptonshire is administered by West Northamptonshire Council and North Northamptonshire Council. Relevant local plan policies and published housing land supply evidence are reviewed for each site, alongside the settlement pattern, access and local constraints.
