Land Opportunities in Northamptonshire

Residential development land and property considered by Abel Gray

Our Northamptonshire Focus

Abel Gray acquires land for residential development across Northamptonshire. We consider land both with and without planning permission, including agricultural land, brownfield property, gardens, paddocks, development plots and redundant buildings.

We work directly with landowners and can agree an outright acquisition, conditional contract or Option Agreement depending on the site and circumstances. Where a transaction is subject to planning, we can fund and manage the planning process ourselves.

Northamptonshire is administered by West Northamptonshire Council and North Northamptonshire Council. Relevant local plan policies and published housing land supply evidence are reviewed for each site, alongside the settlement pattern, access and local constraints.
