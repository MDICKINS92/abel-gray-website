Sell Your Barn

Redundant barns and farm buildings with conversion potential

Discuss a Barn or Farm Building

Abel Gray considers redundant barns, farm buildings and related property in Buckinghamshire, Bedfordshire and Northamptonshire. We assess each opportunity individually, including its condition, setting, access and planning context.

Where an opportunity is suitable, we can discuss a planning-led route, a conditional purchase or an outright acquisition. Initial conversations are confidential and based on the particular circumstances of the property.

What We Consider

Traditional Barns

Stone, brick and timber-framed barns with an established character and potential future use.

Redundant Farm Buildings

Agricultural buildings and outbuildings no longer required for their existing use.

Buildings with Land

Barns with adjoining land where the wider setting may form part of the assessment.
