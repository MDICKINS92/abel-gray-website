Land Opportunities Around Towcester

Abel Gray acquires land for residential development across the Towcester area. We consider land both with and without planning permission, including agricultural land, brownfield property, gardens, paddocks, development plots and redundant buildings.

We work directly with landowners and can agree an outright acquisition, conditional contract or Option Agreement depending on the site and circumstances. Where a transaction is subject to planning, we can fund and manage the planning process ourselves.

West Northamptonshire Council is the local planning authority. Its local plan policy and published housing land supply evidence are reviewed alongside access, services, heritage context and the rural landscape of each site.

Local Context

South-west Northamptonshire combines historic villages, working countryside and strong links to Towcester, Silverstone and Milton Keynes. Opportunities commonly include village-edge paddocks, rural buildings, garden land and under-used property. Field View House in Paulerspury is a Reserved Abel Gray home in the area.
