Land Opportunities Around Towcester

South-west Northamptonshire runs from Towcester out to Silverstone, Blisworth and the Buckinghamshire border, and we look at land throughout it. Village-edge paddocks, gardens, farmland, redundant buildings and consented plots are all of interest, with or without planning in place.

Terms are whatever suits the site and the owner: an outright purchase, a conditional contract, or an Option Agreement. Where planning is needed, we cover the cost of the application and the professional team.

West Northamptonshire Council is the local planning authority. Its local plan policy and published housing land supply evidence are reviewed alongside access, services, heritage context and the rural landscape of each site.

Local Context
