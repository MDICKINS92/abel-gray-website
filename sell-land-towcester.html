Land Opportunities Around Towcester

Abel Gray considers land and property with residential development potential around Towcester and south-west Northamptonshire. We work directly with owners and welcome a confidential discussion about sites with or without planning permission.

Every site is assessed individually, including its setting, access, planning context and potential for a well-considered residential scheme.

Areas We Cover

Our Towcester area coverage includes Towcester, Paulerspury, Greens Norton, Silverstone, Blisworth, Stoke Bruerne, Cosgrove, Old Stratford, Roade, Yardley Gobion, Pury End, Grafton Regis and Hartwell.
