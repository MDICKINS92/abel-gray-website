Our Bedfordshire Focus

We consider a limited number of land and property opportunities across Bedfordshire, including gardens, redundant buildings, previously used sites and land adjoining established settlements.

Planning authority responsibilities vary across the county, including Central Bedfordshire Council and Bedford Borough Council. We review the relevant local policy and published housing land supply evidence for the individual location before deciding whether to progress a discussion.

What We Consider

Small Residential Sites

Infill plots, garden land and land with an appropriate relationship to existing development.

Previously Used Land

Under-used property and redundant buildings with the potential for a considered new purpose.
