Residential development land and property considered by Abel Gray

Our Bedfordshire Focus

We buy residential development land throughout Bedfordshire, from individual building plots to larger village sites. Planning consent is not a requirement. We regularly look at farmland, gardens, paddocks, former commercial property and rural buildings with conversion potential.

How the purchase is structured is up to you. Some landowners prefer a clean unconditional sale, others prefer a conditional contract or an Option Agreement so they share in the value if consent is granted. Where a site needs planning, we fund the application and the professional team at our own risk.

Bedfordshire is split between two authorities. Central Bedfordshire Council covers Leighton Buzzard, Dunstable, Toddington, Shefford and the surrounding villages. Bedford Borough Council covers Bedford and its rural parishes. We check the relevant local plan and housing land supply position before making any proposal.
